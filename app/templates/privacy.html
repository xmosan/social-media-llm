Privacy Policy

Updated October 13, 2026

Information we use

Sabeel Studio stores your account details, workspace membership, brand preferences, prompts, selected sources, drafts, generated content, schedules and publishing history. We also retain feedback you send and operational records used to diagnose errors and protect the service.

Social connections are optional

You can create, save and export content without connecting a social account. When you authorize a supported connection, we use the account identifiers, profile details and access credentials returned by that service to provide the publishing features you choose.

For Facebook and Instagram, this includes the Pages and professional Instagram accounts you authorize. Our Pinterest integration uses your authorized profile and board information to let you choose where to publish, and Pin identifiers to track publishing results. Pinterest connections and manual publishing are available to enabled Sabeel workspaces. We do not request access to your private messages or advertising accounts for these publishing features.

AI and service providers

When you request AI assistance, the relevant prompt, source and creative instructions are sent to OpenAI to generate the requested writing or imagery. Social account credentials are not part of those generation requests. Sabeel does not use connected Pinterest account, board or Pin data to train AI models.

We use Railway to host the application and database, Cloudinary to host media, storage providers for backups, and email and monitoring services to operate and support Sabeel. These services process the information needed for their functions. We do not sell your personal information.

Images and publishing

Generated and uploaded images intended for preview, download or publishing use public media URLs. Anyone with a media URL may be able to view that image. Do not put confidential information in publishable images. Content is sent to a connected social platform when you choose to publish or when a schedule you authorized runs. The destination platform then handles that content under its own policies.

Security, retention and your choices

Our public service uses HTTPS, and account and workspace checks restrict access to private drafts and settings. Access is not a guarantee against every security risk.

Saved work and publishing records remain available for draft recovery and account support. Disconnecting a social account stops future use of that connection; it does not delete posts already published on the social platform or your saved Sabeel drafts. Backup copies may remain until they expire under the applicable backup schedule.

You can revoke a connection in the destination platform's settings. To request access, correction, disconnection or deletion of your Sabeel account data, contact admin@sabeelstudio.com. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Published social posts may also need to be removed on the destination platform.
